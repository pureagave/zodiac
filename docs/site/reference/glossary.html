Reference

Glossary

Terms that appear across the three codebases and are not obvious from context. Physical places, Burning Man vocabulary, and the project's own coinages.

Black Rock City

TermMeans
BRCBlack Rock City — the temporary city built for Burning Man, laid out as a partial annulus of clock-position radials and lettered ring roads.
Golden SpikeThe surveyed centre point of the city, where the Man stands. GoldenSpike.ACTIVE is the app's single source of truth for the active year; it is the origin of the map projection.
Clock positionAn address's angular coordinate, e.g. 2:15. The 12:00 axis is 45.0° true, and did not change when the 2026 city moved ~583 m south-west.
Ring / ring letterThe concentric streets, Esplanade then A through K. StreetRingRadiiM holds each one's measured mean centreline radius, from Esplanade 761.5 m to K 1753 m.
EsplanadeThe innermost ring road, facing the open playa.
PlayaThe open, unbuilt dry lakebed inside and around the city. Also the alkaline dust that gets into everything.
CPNCivic Plaza / Point of interest node in the Innovate GIS — the dataset layer the Temple's coordinate comes from.
Trash fenceThe pentagonal boundary of the event, and one of the rendered map layers.
Innovate GISBurning Man's published geographic dataset (burningmantech/innovate-GIS-data) — streets, plazas, blocks, trash fence, toilets, CPNs. The base map is bundled from it.
BM APIapi.burningman.org — art and camp records, fetched offline-first and cached. Placements are embargoed until roughly three weeks pre-event.
Mutant vehicle / art carA licensed, radically modified vehicle. Zodiac is one.

Project terms

TermMeans
ConceptOne of the three runtime-switchable cockpit UIs — RADAR, MAP or DRIVER. Switching is purely presentational; all three read the same state.
Fleet busThe two fixed multicast channels on the vehicle's own WiFi that all three parts agree on. Detail.
EgoThe vehicle itself, as drawn on the map — the ego marker, the ego fix.
ContactOne detected person on the ZTHREAT channel: a track id, a bearing, an apparent size, and a collision flag.
relAzDegA contact's bearing relative to the vehicle's nose. Negative is left, positive is right, full circle ±180.
sizeApparent size, 0 (far) to 1 (near). A sensor prominence proxy, not a distance — which is why the HUD ring uses ordinal bands rather than a metric radius.
BlipOne mark on the DRIVER surround ring. May represent a cluster of contacts sharing a band and within 15°.
Blind arc / covered arcBearings the camera rig does or does not watch. An uncovered sector must never render like a watched-and-clear one.
Vision feed stateLIVE / DEMO / ABSENT. The word CLEAR may only appear when LIVE.
Drive-toThe active navigation target: HOME, MAN, TEMPLE, BATH or a typed address.
Ops readoutThe themed footer on RADAR and MAP — clock, sun, beacon health, drive-to guidance.
Burn-in phaseACTIVE / DIM / DEEP_IDLE / SLEEP. Detail.
Passenger roleA per-device flag that swaps the cockpit for the rider-facing card carousel. Hidden in both directions.
Weak labelA motion-detector box recorded alongside a training frame. Not truth — its value is that correcting a box is far faster than drawing one.
Golden corpusThe measured cross-language ZTHREAT test vectors that both suites read. Detail.

Hardware and protocol terms

TermMeans
NMEA 0183The GPS sentence format the beacon speaks. $ + body + * + two hex XOR checksum digits + CRLF.
GGA / RMC / HDTThe three standard sentences the tablet consumes: position + fix quality, position + speed + course, and true heading.
VTG / HDG / HDMSentences the tablet deliberately rejects — VTG is GPS course not compass heading; HDG/HDM are magnetic. Why.
LWIRLong-wave infrared — what a thermal camera sees. Glass blocks it entirely, which is why the pod needs a germanium window.
FFCFlat-field correction — the Lepton's internal shutter closing every few minutes. The frame goes uniform for an instant. Normal, not a fault, and guarded against by ReBaselineGuard.
RadiometricA thermal camera that reports absolute temperature. The Ultra Wide is non-radiometric, which is fine — detection is by contrast and motion.
UVCUSB Video Class — a driverless camera standard. Both cameras enumerate as /dev/videoN.
DMX512The lighting control protocol. One universe is 512 channels; the moving head occupies 11 of them.
oladThe Open Lighting Architecture daemon. zvision HTTP-POSTs a universe to it; it owns the FTDI dongle and the frame timing.
GoboA patterned disc in the beam path of a moving head. The tracker deliberately stays on the open slot.
Pan / tilt fineThe low byte of a 16-bit pan or tilt value. 8-bit pan across 540° is ~2.1° per step, visibly steppy on a slow follow — the fine channels are the whole reason to run 11-channel mode.
JetPack / L4TNVIDIA's Jetson OS distribution and its Linux-for-Tegra base. The box runs JetPack 7.2 / L4T 39.2.
MAXN_SUPERThe Orin Nano Super's full power mode. Mode 2 on JetPack 7.2, not 0 — the board ships on 1.
TensorRT engineA compiled inference binary. Not portable across GPU architectures — export ONNX on the server, compile on the Orin.
