Protocols · the seams

The fleet bus

The three parts of Zodiac share no code. They agree through two cross-codebase wire formats on the fleet bus — NMEA and ZTHREAT — plus a third channel, $ZNAV, over which the tablets share one piece of state among themselves. This section is the byte-level reference — it is the most valuable document in the repository, because it is the only place the codebases actually meet.

The channels

GroupPortSenderReceivers
Telemetry (NMEA)239.7.7.1010110:beaconEvery :app tablet; zvision's $ZAUD listener
Threats (ZTHREAT)239.7.7.2010120zvisionEvery :app tablet
Shared nav target ($ZNAV)239.7.7.3010130A nav-authority :app tabletEvery :app tablet

Multicast TTL is 1 — nothing leaves the vehicle's subnet. 239.0.0.0/8 is administratively scoped.

$ZNAV is the newest channel and the only app-to-app one: the fleet's drive-to destination, so every tablet's DRIVER HUD points the same way. It is single-language (:app only), so unlike NMEA and ZTHREAT it carries no cross-language drift risk. A fourth channel, $ZVER (239.7.7.40:10140), is in progress for fleet build-version reporting. See shared destination and the bus-channel status table below.

THREAT_GROUP    = 239.7.7.20   THREAT_PORT    = 10120
NAV_GROUP       = 239.7.7.30   NAV_PORT       = 10130    ($ZNAV, :app <-> :app)
VER_GROUP       = 239.7.7.40   VER_PORT       = 10140    ($ZVER, in progress)

Every sender transmits twice

Each datagram goes out once to the multicast group and once to the /24 subnet-directed broadcast (falling back to 255.255.255.255 if the local IP cannot be determined). Some access points silently drop multicast, and a fleet that goes blind because of an AP setting is not acceptable.

Every receiver binds the wildcard address on the port and joins the group, so it gets whichever copy arrives. The consequence is duplicate delivery — which is why the tablet de-duplicates byte-identical $ZSHK lines within 200 ms.

Android multicast gotcha, handled

The WiFi driver filters broadcast and multicast frames from apps to save power unless the app holds a WifiManager.MulticastLock (with CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE). Both NetworkLocationSource and NetworkThreatSource hold one.

The two formats

  • :beacon → :app

    NMEA telemetry

    Standard NMEA 0183 framing. Raw GNSS forwarded verbatim, plus seven synthesized sentence types carrying heading, IMU, mic level, lux, shock, beacon health and the odometer.

    Read the field tables →

  • zvision → :app

    ZTHREAT frames

    One UDP datagram is one frame of contacts. Not NMEA: no leading $, no checksum, an explicitly pinned numeric grammar, and a measured golden corpus that both test suites read.
